A dense newspaper front page summing up Tuesday 24 November 2026 in one image:
33 stories across 7 regions, every one first reported on 24 November 2026,
with a UTC timeline and a markets strip.

- `main.py` — pure stdlib Python: own 5x7 bitmap font, own PNG encoder.
  Renders `entry.png` (1024x1024, deterministic) into `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`.
- `entry.png` — the front page: masthead THE WORLD TODAY, lead on Ukraine,
  six region panels (Europe, Middle East, Africa, Asia & Pacific, Americas,
  Sport), markets strip and a day-in-numbers band.
